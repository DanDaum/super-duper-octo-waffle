Feedback & Updates

Thank you for any feedback—positive or critical. It helps us prioritize fixes and improvements. We regularly ship updates to improve performance, polish the experience, and address bugs.

  • Bug reports: Tell us what happened and what you expected.
  • Feature requests: Tell us what you’re trying to accomplish.
  • Device info (optional): iPhone/iPad model and iOS version can help reproduce issues.

Please avoid sending sensitive personal information in email.

Quick Tips

  • Make sure you’re on the latest app version from the App Store.
  • Try closing and reopening the app if something looks stuck.
  • Restarting your device can resolve some temporary issues.
